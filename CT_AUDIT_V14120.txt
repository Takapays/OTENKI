V1.4.120 CT information reduction / nationwide expansion

Policy change
- Do not return CT_INFO_NONE merely because multiple confirmed trail paths exist.
- When several confirmed composed paths connect the same two selected points, choose one representative path.
- Selection order: source quality -> fewer segments -> shorter standard CT.
- Official/public mountain sources remain highest priority.
- Among private mountain services, Yamakei / Yamareco are prioritized over YAMAP.
- Yamareco segments from different plans are still never mixed together.
- Composed route search expanded from 6 to 8 edges, capped at 24 hours.
- Composed CT results are cached in-browser for repeated point edits.

Graph audit after V1.4.120
- direct fixed CT edges: 559

- composable pairs without a direct fixed edge: 1,009
- multi-path pairs now eligible for representative-route selection: >400
