Base: V1.6.119 low-egress only. Update the service and both jobs to the same image. No forced refresh or cache deletion, no environment, schedule, TTL or cache-engine changes.

- Nationwide candidate-day grade: retain previous A-C diagnostic rules; a serious single wind/rain risk or a short compound event is normally D. E when JMA/GFS corroborate severe wind (ridge >=9 m/s or estimated gust >=18 m/s) and rain >=1.5 mm/h at the same time for >=5 of 10 slots (06-15 JST), or both forecast strong wind (>=15 or estimated gust >=25) with MET-led temperature <=-10 C for >=5 slots. Count unique hours, not gust as a separate risk. These are trial classification thresholds, not validated safety limits.
- Exceptional E without duration requirement: ridge >=25 or derived gust >=37.5 m/s, surface >=20 or actual non-GFS gust >=28 m/s, precipitation >=10 mm/h, high thunder indicator or visibility <=100 m if present. An incomplete comparison retains E when the prior E cannot be ruled out; existing WeatherAPI safety overlay remains.

Existing saved series are reused for reclassification, and Instagram reads use the same rule. Different forecast inputs may still differ.
